# Popover

The interactive floating card on the cdk popup — the **Tooltip's interactive
sibling**. Where the Tooltip is a click-through hint, the Popover holds real
controls in a **non-modal dialog** panel (buttons, inputs, lists) that stays
interactive under the pointer and the keyboard. Popover renders **no wrapper
element** — it clones its trigger in place, exactly where the author wrote it,
and mounts the panel out of a body portal.

<CodeBlock className="colox-import" language="tsx">{`import { Popover } from '@colox/react';
import '@colox/react/style.css';`}</CodeBlock>

## Channels

One name, two channels — and giving both is a compile-time error, never a
priority:

- **Props form**: a single trigger child plus the `title`/`content` props.
  Falsy content (the empty string, `null`, `false`) renders no panel — an
  empty-content popover never opens, clicks included (the aria surface says
  `expanded=false` and stays honest).
- **Composed form**: `<Popover.Trigger>` (exactly one; its single child — a
  component or a bare element — becomes the trigger) plus at most one
  `<Popover.Title>` (the panel header) and at most one `<Popover.Content>`
  (the panel body) for custom DOM. `className`/`style` on the Title/Content
  parts are the escape hatch and land on the header/the content box. Composed
  mode without a Content part renders the plain trigger.

Popover injects the interaction surfaces (events) plus the dialog wiring:

- `aria-haspopup="dialog"` always,
- a live `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` (the panel id appended to an
  existing author word) while open,
- the panel is `role="dialog"` with `aria-labelledby` pointing at the title
  row (only when a title exists).

The trigger element keeps its own props — the author's `onClick` chains after
the popover's, `className` concatenates.

```tsx
<Popover title="Account" content="The props form.">
  <Button>Open me</Button>
</Popover>

<Popover>
  <Popover.Trigger>
    <Button>Custom body</Button>
  </Popover.Trigger>
  <Popover.Title>Account</Popover.Title>
  <Popover.Content>
    <Input placeholder="Type something" />
  </Popover.Content>
</Popover>
```

## When to use

- Rich interactive cards anchored to a trigger — filters, quick forms, menus.
- Any place a `Tooltip` would be wrong because the content must be clickable.
- Custom bodies: the composed channel keeps the trigger and the panel DOM in
  your hands, no wrapper in between.

## Surface

The surface is an **OPAQUE card** — unlike the Tooltip's translucent glass,
an interactive reading surface needs full contrast:

- `--colox-color-bg-default` fill — dark mode flips the card automatically;
- **borderless**, `radius-lg` — the shadow defines the edge;
- the depth is a **union drop-shadow cast on the panel itself** (the
  ant-design approach, same recipe as the Tooltip): the card and the
  protruding arrow half silhouette together in one pass at the Tooltip's
  calibration (4px offset, 6px blur) — a heavier cast parks its darkest
  mass on the panel edge and reads as a seam between the white arrow and
  the white card, so the hint layer's recipe wins — the offset follows
  the arrow direction, and the color is the design language's own shadow
  value (rgba(25, 25, 25, 0.10) — the master alpha carried by the
  `--colox-shadow-*` tokens);
- **no backdrop blur** — the hint layer's frost stays a Tooltip concern;
- the arrow is the same **rotated opaque diamond half buried behind the
  card** — no clip-path, only the protruding tip corner rounded — on the
  card fill, pinned to the **resolved** placement and aimed at the trigger
  under boundary collisions;
- **width = the content's own** — no size axis (the DatePicker precedent).
  An upper bound is the consumer's CSS escape hatch.

<Popover title="Account" content="The opaque card.">
  <Button>Open</Button>
</Popover>
<Popover title="Other side" content="Below the trigger." placement="bottom">
  <Button>Bottom</Button>
</Popover>
<Popover title="No arrow" content="The gap tightens." showArrow={false}>
  <Button>Arrowless</Button>
</Popover>

## Behavior

`visibleOn` picks the trigger channel:

- `"click"` (default): an instant toggle. Opening **moves the keyboard focus
  into the panel** (the first focusable element, or the panel itself when the
  content holds none); while the focus lives inside, **Tab and Shift+Tab
  cycle within the panel** (a non-modal dialog keeps its keystrokes);
  **Escape closes and returns the focus to the trigger**; an outside
  pointerdown closes and the focus follows the click naturally. A forward
  **Tab on the trigger slides into the panel** (the portal mounts at the
  document end, outside the natural tab order).
- `"hover"`: pointer enter opens after `delay.in` (300ms) with an instant
  focus leg — focusing opens immediately. The close rides `delay.out`
  (100ms) — **the out-delay is the pointer bridge**: the panel is
  interactive, so the pointer crossing from the trigger into the panel must
  not drop it; entering the panel cancels the pending close. A trigger blur
  whose focus landed **inside the panel** keeps it open (internal
  navigation). **Hover never steals focus** — pointer interaction doesn't
  rob the keyboard. The open and close timers always cancel each other.
- `"manual"`: the controlled word — `visible` drives the panel; interactions
  never auto-open or auto-close, and `onVisibleChange` stays silent (it only
  speaks the hover/click transitions).

`closeOnScroll` closes on any scroll while open — an explicit opt-out; the
default follows the trigger through the popup's auto-update stream instead.
`delay` merges partial objects into the defaults (`{ out: 200 }` keeps the
300ms open delay).

## Props

| Prop            | Type                             | Default          |
| --------------- | -------------------------------- | ---------------- |
| title           | `ReactNode`                      | —                |
| content         | `ReactNode`                      | —                |
| visibleOn       | `'click' \| 'hover' \| 'manual'` | `'click'`        |
| visible         | `boolean`                        | —                |
| showArrow       | `boolean`                        | `true`           |
| placement       | `Placement`                      | `'bottom-start'` |
| delay           | `{ in?, out? }`                  | `{ 300, 100 }`   |
| closeOnScroll   | `boolean`                        | `false`          |
| onVisibleChange | `(visible: boolean) => void`     | —                |

## Boundaries

- **The trigger must accept a ref.** A component or host element without ref
  support can't be referenced — no invisible span fallback is injected.
- **The exit window.** On close the panel stays mounted for the fade-out
  (`--colox-motion-duration-fast`, 100ms); a re-open inside that window
  reuses the same panel node.
- **Width is content-owned.** There is no size axis; an upper bound is the
  consumer's CSS escape hatch.
- **No `defaultVisible`.** The click/hover channels are presentation-only —
  first-show behavior needs no initial word, and the manual channel is
  controlled.
